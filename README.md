# The Practice Desk

A plain-English guide to five automated **paper-trading** strategies running on a Raspberry Pi —
real live market prices, imaginary money, honest bookkeeping.

Read it: **https://realtalkmasi.github.io/TradeBot/**

The page covers how markets work from zero, what each of the five strategies bets on, a
step-by-step walkthrough of a single real trade, the risk controls, the current scoreboard, and
what's been learned so far — including the part where simply buying an index fund and doing
nothing has beaten all five strategies.

## Why publish the losses

Most trading content shows the wins. This shows the whole ledger, including a strategy that lost
10% of its pretend account in one afternoon. The failures are where the learning is.
